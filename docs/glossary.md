# Glossary

Plain-English terms used in this project, in alphabetical order.

**Acquisition.** When a company buys another company. Atlas Copco grows this way (29 announced in 2025), which is why we watch the market for companies and technology.

**Aftercooler.** A cooler fitted after the compressor that removes heat, and so much of the water, from freshly compressed air.

**Air dryer.** Equipment that removes water vapour from compressed air so it does not damage tools or products. Common types are refrigerated and desiccant dryers.

**Air end.** The part of a compressor that actually compresses the air (for a screw compressor, the pair of screws in their housing). The motor and controls drive it.

**Air receiver.** A tank that stores compressed air so the compressor does not have to start and stop for every small demand.

**Atlas Copco.** Our client. A Swedish industrial group with four business areas: Compressor Technique, Vacuum Technique, Power Technique and Industrial Technique.

**Bar.** A metric unit of pressure. About 14.5 psi.

**CAGI.** Compressed Air and Gas Institute, a US industry body for compressed air.

**Centrifugal compressor.** A compressor that spins an impeller at high speed to push air outward. Used for very large, steady air demand.

**CFM.** Cubic feet per minute: how much air a compressor delivers. The usual flow rating in the US (m3/min in metric).

**Claim.** A statement a company makes about itself, such as a marketing line in its own news post. Our default label when unsure. See also *Fact*.

**Compliance Gate.** The step in our pipeline that decides whether a source or page may be visited at all. It checks the source's status, the site's terms, and robots.txt, and enforces a polite delay.

**Compressed air.** Ordinary air squeezed into a smaller volume so it carries energy. Factories use it for tools, machinery and processes.

**Compressor Technique.** Atlas Copco's largest business area (about 46% of 2025 revenue), covering compressors and related equipment.

**Crawl-delay.** A robots.txt setting asking automated visitors to wait a number of seconds between requests.

**Databricks.** The cloud platform where we store all results. Nothing is stored on our laptops.

**Desiccant dryer.** An air dryer that uses a drying material to absorb moisture, used where very dry air is needed.

**Evidence record.** One collected item with its source link, collection date, licence note, and fact or claim label. The unit of our output.

**Fact.** A statement made by a neutral or official source (a regulator, or a reporter reporting an event). Compare *Claim*.

**Filter (relevance filter).** Four yes/no checks on every item: is it about compressors, in North America, from a credible source, and recent? An item passes only if all four are yes, and the reasons are stored.

**Fingerprint.** A short code computed from a page's text. If the code is the same next time, the page has not changed and can be skipped. We keep the fingerprint, not the text.

**hp / kW.** Horsepower and kilowatts: the motor size of a compressor (1 hp is about 0.75 kW).

**Item.** One article or news post our pipeline collected and turned into a record.

**JSON.** A simple text format for structured data. Our output is JSON.

**Market & Technology study.** Our project's name: helping Atlas Copco see who is building what, so its team can spot relevant technology and companies sooner.

**Niche.** The domain we focus on. Ours is industrial compressor technology.

**Oil-free compressor.** A compressor that never lets oil touch the air being compressed. Needed in food, pharmaceutical and electronics work.

**Personal data (PII).** Names, emails, phone numbers and similar details of real people. We remove them before keeping anything.

**PoC (proof of concept).** A small working version that proves the idea. Friday's demo is a PoC.

**psi.** Pounds per square inch: the usual US pressure unit.

**Reciprocating compressor.** A compressor with pistons moving back and forth, like an engine.

**robots.txt.** A file on a website that tells automated visitors which pages they may visit. We obey it every time.

**Rotary screw compressor.** The most common industrial compressor: two meshing screws squeeze the air. Runs continuously.

**RSS feed.** A machine-readable list of a site's newest posts. We prefer it to reading web pages.

**Scout.** Our word for a program that visits approved websites on a schedule and brings back new items that match a filter.

**Scroll compressor.** A compressor with two spiral-shaped parts, one orbiting the other. Quiet, used for smaller needs.

**Source register.** The list of websites we may read, with a status for each. It lives in `config/sources.yaml`.

**VSD (variable speed drive).** A motor controller that lets a compressor run at the speed the factory needs, saving energy.

**Volume (Databricks).** A cloud folder in Databricks where our files are stored.

# Keyword suggestions

Proposed additions to `config/keywords.yaml`. Terms already in the file are not repeated. Matching is case-insensitive, whole word or phrase.

## compressor
- oil-injected — common alternative to oil-free; items about lubricated screw compressors
- oil-flooded — other name for oil-injected screw compressors
- two-stage — two-stage compression is a typical efficiency feature
- air compressor — the most common phrase in headlines (the single word "compressor" already matches, so this is optional)
- aftercooler — equipment that comes with compressor systems
- desiccant dryer — air-treatment equipment
- refrigerated dryer — air-treatment equipment
- compressed air system — whole-system articles
- air leak — leak detection and repair programmes are a frequent topic
- leak detection — same
- heat recovery — energy-saving feature of compressors
- permanent magnet motor — efficient drive technology
- IE4 — high motor efficiency class
- booster compressor — higher-pressure add-on units
- nitrogen generator — adjacent product from the same makers
- blower — related product line (for example Kaeser blowers)
- ISO 8573 — the international standard for compressed air purity
- ISO 1217 — the standard for testing compressor performance
- SCFM — standard cubic feet per minute, a flow rating in US articles
- psig — pressure unit used in US articles
- energy efficiency — recurring theme in trade news (also appears outside compressors, see risks below)
- predictive maintenance — monitoring and connected-compressor news

## terms that might cause false matches
- plant — already in `industrial`; also means a living plant, and "to plant" in other contexts
- process — very generic ("legal process", "hiring process"); consider removing it from `industrial`
- energy efficiency — appears in buildings, appliances and utilities news, not only compressors
- facility — generic (also hospital or office facilities)
- american / america — appear in company and organisation names that are not about US location (for example a name that only contains "American")
- blower — also leaf blowers and hair blowers
- booster — also vaccine boosters and rocket boosters
- Georgia — is also a country, so a "Georgia" mention may not mean the US state
- Washington — is also a very common person's name and a government shorthand
- Charlotte, Houston — are also first names or surnames
- mining — also "data mining" in unrelated technology news
- oil-free — also appears in cooking and cosmetics
- compressor — also refrigerators, air conditioners and "compression" of data; the other compressor keywords usually settle which is meant
